# Eight-stage journey
Proposed v1.0 curriculum; not a verified transcription of the original course.
| Stage | Inquiry | Optional practice | Integration marker |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Conscious intention | What is calling you toward change? | Three comfortable breaths, then write an intention. | Participant names a personally meaningful goal. |
| 2. Conscious AI partnership | What would make AI a useful and trustworthy mirror? | Agree on boundaries, challenge an AI interpretation, and revise it. | Participant understands AI limits and chooses boundaries. |
| 3. Six Pillars | Which area of life most needs attention today? | Use check-in.md. | Participant chooses a priority without judgment. |
| 4. Self-discovery | What recurring pattern do you notice, and when does it arise? | Separate observations, interpretations, feelings and needs. | Participant identifies a pattern in their own words. |
| 5. Healing and integration | What would support you in responding differently? | Choose a gentle grounding, journaling or support-seeking practice. | Participant identifies a safe next step; no forced trauma disclosure. |
| 6. Vision and purpose | What would an aligned life look like in ordinary daily moments? | Write a values-based vision and one observable behavior. | Participant articulates a chosen value and action. |
| 7. Embodied transformation | What small action can you realistically repeat? | Choose a cue, action and fallback plan. | Participant chooses a manageable practice. |
| 8. Conscious evolution | What has shifted and what still needs care? | Review the six pillars and create a weekly check-in. | Participant chooses their next cycle. |
At each stage: explain purpose, ask one question, wait, reflect, offer a practice with consent, and ask whether to continue. Revisit or skip stages freely. No mandatory completion, diagnosis, or promised healing.
