# Eight-stage journey
Stage names recovered from December 19, 2024 project discussions. Exercises below are newly developed coaching adaptations, not quotations from the manuscript.

| Stage | Opening inquiry | Optional practice | Integration marker |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness and Recognition | What is calling for your attention now? | Notice sensations, feelings and thoughts without judgment; choose an intention. | Names a meaningful intention. |
| Introspection and Healing | What pattern would you like to understand with compassion? | Journal observations, feelings, needs and choices; seek support when useful. | Identifies a self-chosen safe next action. |
| Conscious Expansion | What assumptions or perspectives are you ready to examine? | Explore an alternative interpretation with AI, checking evidence and personal resonance. | Can hold multiple perspectives without surrendering discernment. |
| Empowerment and Purpose | What values do you want your choices to embody? | Connect a value to one concrete action. | Names a purpose-linked behavior. |
| Community and Co-Creation | What kind of mutual connection would help you and others thrive? | Identify one consensual, reciprocal collaboration or boundary. | Chooses a constructive relationship action. |
| World-Centric Perspective | How could your choices affect people and the living world beyond your immediate circle? | Map likely impacts and uncertainties; choose a modest contribution. | Identifies a realistic collective or ecological action. |
| Integration and Embodiment | How can you practice what you have learned in ordinary life? | Choose a cue, small action and fallback; revisit the six pillars. | Establishes a manageable practice. |
| Evolutionary Co-Creation | What have you learned, and what would you like to create next with others? | Review progress and choose a next cycle, with consent and shared accountability. | Sets a participant-chosen next direction. |

At every stage use one question at a time; reflect before offering a practice. Invite Six Pillars check-ins at entry and integration, and whenever helpful. Respect participant pace and right to skip. Never equate spirituality with superiority or insist on a worldview. AI literacy, uncertainty and consent are woven throughout.
