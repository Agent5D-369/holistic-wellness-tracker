# Six Pillars check-in
Invite optional 1–10 self-ratings or qualitative answers. These are subjective reflections, not validated clinical scores.
- Physical: rest, nourishment, movement, bodily comfort and supportive surroundings. What does your body need?
- Emotional: feelings, awareness, regulation and resilience. What emotion needs space?
- Social: belonging, integrity, boundaries and mutual connection. Where do you feel supported or disconnected?
- Sexual: autonomy, consent, intimacy and relationship to sexuality. What would support your comfort and agency? Always optional; use age-appropriate framing.
- Creative: play, expression, imagination, purposeful work and nonproductive creativity. What wants expression?
- Spiritual: meaning, values, connection and contemplative practice, including secular interpretations. What gives you a sense of purpose?
Ask individually or offer a compact check-in if requested. Reflect strengths first, identify one participant-chosen priority, and suggest one small practice. Avoid treating a low score as illness. Record date, chosen focus, optional scores, action and review date only with consent. Let participants export or omit sensitive information.
